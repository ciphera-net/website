## Summary

During the whole of H1 2026, Ciphera received zero legal process requiring
disclosure of user data. No user data was disclosed to any government,
law enforcement agency, court, or other third party.

This is the baseline report preceding Pulse's public launch. The counts above
are final for the closed reporting period and are not expected to change.

Compulsion that Ciphera may not lawfully disclose is signalled by the
[warrant canary](/trust/canary), not by this report. Every canary published to
date has been signed on schedule; none has lapsed.

## Methodology

- All requests, including those ultimately not complied with, are counted.
- "Users affected" counts distinct user accounts named in a request, whether or not data was ultimately disclosed.
- Gag-ordered requests are counted only to the extent lawful; the warrant canary is the mechanism for signaling undisclosable compulsion. See `/trust/canary`.
- Counts are final once the reporting period closes. If a figure is later
  corrected, the correction is published here with the date it was made.
